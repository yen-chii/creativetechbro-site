FAQ

Do I need to be technical?

No. I translate everything into plain language and build around the tools your team already uses.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped after a free discovery call, with a fixed quote before any work starts. Discovery Sprints are the lowest-commitment way in.

Which AI tools do you use?

Whatever fits the job: the major AI models, automation platforms like Zapier, Make and n8n, and custom code where off-the-shelf won't cut it. I'm not tied to any vendor.

What about our data?

Data handling is part of every plan. I'll flag what can and can't go into AI tools, and design around your privacy and client obligations.

Do you work outside London?

Yes. Most work is remote, with in-person sessions in London.
